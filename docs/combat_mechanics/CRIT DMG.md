# CRIT DMG

**Type:** Combat

Stats is a character-only stat in Honkai: Star Rail . It increases the DMG dealt when an attack scores a CRIT hit. Note that DoT , True DMG , and Weakness Break -related DMG cannot CRIT. Characters have a base CRIT DMG of 50% .
