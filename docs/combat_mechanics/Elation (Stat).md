Elation . Elation Stats A base stat that affects Elation DMG and can boost the multiplier for Elation DMG dealt. Elation is a character-only stat in Honkai: Star Rail . It increases Elation DMG dealt by a percentage. Characters following the Path of Elation can obtain Elation Boosts from Trace .
