# Game Systems

**Type:** Combat

There are seven Types in Honkai: Star Rail : Physical , Fire , Ice , Lightning , Wind , Quantum , and Imaginary . Each playable ally is assigned a single Type and their attacks deal matching DMG. Enemies typically have to Types. Attacks of a Type they are weak to will reduce their Toughness . When the enemy's Toughness is fully depleted, they will suffer a Weakness Break, taking an instance of Break DMG, likely receiving a debuff corresponding to the Type that caused the Weakness Break, and delaying their action. Such an enemy will stay in the Weakness Broken state until the start of their next turn, during which they will take 11.111...% more DMG. Allies do not have Toughness, and are unable to be inflicted with Weakness Break. However, Type-related debuffs can still be applied to allies by certain enemy attacks.
