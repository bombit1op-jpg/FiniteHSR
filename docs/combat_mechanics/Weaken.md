# Weaken

**Type:** Combat

Stats is a hidden stat in Honkai: Star Rail that is applied by the debuff . It decreases DMG dealt by a percentage, and can be applied to both characters and enemies. Note that Weakness Break -related DMG and Elation DMG are not affected by Weaken. Weaken is applied to DMG dealt with the following multiplier: Weaken Multiplier = − {\displaystyle \text{Weaken Multiplier} = 1 - \text{Weaken}}

## Formulas

**`Weaken Multiplier = 1 - Weaken`**
