# Type

**Type:** Status Effect

Combat Status Effects are a key combat mechanic in Honkai: Star Rail . They are applied to allies and enemies to provide them with certain effects. There are types of Status Effects: Buffs , Debuffs , and Other Effects . 1.2.2

## Formulas

**`align Real Chance = Base Chance \\ × (1 + Effect Hit Rate_Attacker) \\ × (1 - Effect RES_Target) \\ × (1 - Debuff RES_Target) align`**
