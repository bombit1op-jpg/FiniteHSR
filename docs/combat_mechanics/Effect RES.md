# Effect RES

**Type:** Combat

Stats is one of the stats in Honkai: Star Rail , used by both characters and enemies. It decreases the probability of an attack inflicting a debuff on a target, alongside Debuff RES . Real Chance = Base Chance × ( + Effect Hit Rate Attacker ) × ( − Target ) × ( − Debuff RES Target ) {\displaystyle \begin{align} \text{Real Chance} ={} \text{Base Chance} \\ \times (1 + \text{Effect Hit Rate}_\text{Attacker}) \\ \times (1 - \text{Effect RES}_\text{Target}) \\ \times (1 - \text{Debuff RES}_\text{Target}) \end{align}}
