**Type:** Combat

Shield (Item) . Shield Shields are a combat mechanic in Honkai: Star Rail . They are buffs applied to characters that will absorb DMG taken in place of HP. Shield Value = ( Stat × Stat Scaling % + Additive Shield Bonus ) × ( + Shield Bonus % ) {\displaystyle \begin{align} \text{Shield Value} = {} (\text{Stat} \times \text{Stat Scaling} \% + \text{Additive Shield Bonus}) \\ \times (1 + \text{Shield Bonus} \%) \end{align}} The remaining shield value of a character is displayed in the character menu while in combat, alongside HP and Energy . If an instance of DMG exceeds a shield's remaining value, the portion that the shield fails to absorb will overflow and be dealt to the character.
