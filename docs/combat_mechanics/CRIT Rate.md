# CRIT Rate

**Type:** Combat

Stats is a character-only stat in Honkai: Star Rail . It determines the probability of attacks scoring a CRIT Hit, which increases the DMG dealt based on CRIT DMG . Note that DoT and Weakness Break -related DMG cannot CRIT. Characters have a base CRIT Rate of 5% . CRIT Multiplier = { + CRIT DMG if attack scores a CRIT hit otherwise {\displaystyle \text{CRIT Multiplier} = \begin{cases} 1 + \text{CRIT DMG} \text{if attack scores a CRIT hit}\\ 1 \text{otherwise}\\ \end{cases}}
