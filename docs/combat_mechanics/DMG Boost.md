# DMG Boost

**Type:** Combat

Stats DMG Boosts are a type of stats in Honkai: Star Rail , used by both characters and enemies. These stats increase outgoing DMG by a percentage. DMG Boosts may be specific to a Type , a type of attack ( Basic ATK , Skill , Ultimate , DoT , Follow-Up Attack , etc.), or apply to all types of DMG. Different sources of DMG Boosts stack additively. Note that Weakness Break -related DMG and Elation DMG are not affected by DMG Boosts.
