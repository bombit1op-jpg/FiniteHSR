# Damage RES

**Type:** Combat

Stats are stats used by both characters and enemies. These stats will decrease the amount of DMG taken of their corresponding type . RES Multiplier = − ( RES Target − RES PEN Attacker ) {\displaystyle \text{RES Multiplier} = 1 - (\text{RES}_\text{Target} - \text{RES PEN}_\text{Attacker})} Typically, enemies will have 0% RES against types they are weak to and 20% RES against other types. There are a few exceptions to this rule, with these higher RES values usually being given to bosses and themed enemies. Apocalyptic Shadow enemies have a minimum of 40% Damage RES to types they are not weak to. Since Version 4.0 , the maximum base Damage RES possessed by an enemy is 80%. Damage RES ranges between -100% and 90%. Therefore, RES Multiplier ranges between 10% and 200%.

## Formulas

**`RES Multiplier = 1 - (RES_Target - RES PEN_Attacker)`**
