# Damage

**Type:** Combat

, abbreviated in-game as DMG , is dealt when a character or enemy attacks its target.

## Formulas

**`DMG= Base DMG_Attacker× Original DMG Multiplier_Attacker\\ × CRIT Multiplier_Attacker× DMG Boost Multiplier_Attacker\\ × Weaken Multiplier_Attacker\\ × DEF Multiplier_Target× RES Multiplier_Target\\ × Vulnerability Multiplier_Target× DMG Mitigation Multiplier_Target\\ × Broken Multiplier_Target`**

**`Base DMG=Stat× Ability Multiplier+Extra DMG`**

**`Ability Multiplier=Base Ability Multiplier+DMG Multiplier Increase`**

**`CRIT Multiplier= cases1+CRIT DMG if damage can CRIT and rolls a CRIT hit\\1 otherwise\\ cases`**

**`DMG Boost Multiplier=1+Type DMG Boost+Elemental DMG Boost+All DMG Boost`**

**`Type`**

**`Weaken Multiplier = 1 - Weaken`**

**`DEF Multiplier = 1 - ((DEF)/(DEF + 200 + 10 × Level_Attacker))`**

**`DEF = 200 + 10 × Level`**

**`DEF Multiplier= Level_Attacker+20 matrix(Level_Enemy+20)\\× max\0,1+\%DEF Bonus-\%DEF Reduction-\%DEF Ignore\\\+ Level_Attacker+20 matrix`**

**`RES Multiplier = 1 - (RES_Target - RES PEN_Attacker)`**

**`Vulnerability Multiplier=1+Type Vulnerability+Elemental Vulnerability+All-Type Vulnerability`**

**`Type`**

**`DMG Mitigation Multiplier_Total= (1-DMG Mitigation_1)× (1-DMG Mitigation_2)\\× (1-DMG Mitigation_3)×`**

**`Broken Multiplier = cases 0.9 if enemy has Toughness\\ 1.0 if enemy Toughness is broken\\ cases`**
