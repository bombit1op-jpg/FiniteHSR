# Outgoing Healing Boost

**Type:** Combat

Stats is a character-only stat in Honkai: Star Rail . It increases the amount of healing provided by a character by a percentage. Healing Amount = ( Stat × Percentage Healing + Additive Healing ) × Healing Boost Multiplier {\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}{\text{Healing Amount}}={} ({\text{Stat}}\times {\text{Percentage Healing}}+{\text{Additive Healing}})\\ \times {\text{Healing Boost Multiplier}}\end{aligned}}} Where: Healing Boost Multiplier = + Healer + Incoming Healing Boost Target − Incoming Healing Reduction Target {\displaystyle {\begin{aligned}{\text{Healing Boost Multiplier}}={} 1+{\text{Outgoing Healing Boost}}_{\text{Healer}}\\ +{\text{Incoming Healing Boost}}_{\text{Target}}\\ -{\text{Incoming Healing Reduction}}_{\text{Target}}\end{aligned}}}

## Formulas

**`Healing Amount= (Stat× Percentage Healing+Additive Healing)\\ × Healing Boost Multiplier`**

**`Healing Boost Multiplier= 1+Outgoing Healing Boost_Healer\\ +Incoming Healing Boost_Target\\ -Incoming Healing Reduction_Target`**
