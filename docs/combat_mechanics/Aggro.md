Aggro Stats Aggro ( unofficial name ) is one of the stats in Honkai: Star Rail , used by characters and memosprites . It determines a character's probability of being targeted by enemies. Aggro = Base Aggro × ( + Aggro Modifier ) {\displaystyle \text{Aggro} = \text{Base Aggro} \times (1 + \text{Aggro Modifier})} Probability of Being Targeted = Aggro Character ∑ Aggro Team {\displaystyle \text{Probability of Being Targeted} = \frac{\text{Aggro}_\text{Character}}{\sum\text{Aggro}_\text{Team}}} Note that some enemies have Bounce attacks — such attacks are unaffected by Aggro and all characters have an equal chance to get hit. Taunt 4.1

## Formulas

**`Aggro = Base Aggro × (1 + Aggro Modifier)`**

**`Probability of Being Targeted = (Aggro_Character)/( _Team)`**
