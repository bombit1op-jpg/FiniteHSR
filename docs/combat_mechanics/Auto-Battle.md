# Auto-Battle

**Type:** Combat

is a feature in Honkai: Star Rail that automatically controls the player's team during combat. While Auto-Battle is active, usage and targeting of abilities are determined by a variety of factors, including Skill Points, enemy weaknesses , enemy HP , team HP, Status Effects , etc. Auto-Battle is unlocked during Simulated Universe: First Closed Beta , but may become unavailable during certain story battles. It is also unavailable during battles in Aptitude Showcases .
