# Break Effect

**Type:** Combat

Stats is a character-only stat in Honkai: Star Rail . It enhances certain effects caused by Weakness Breaks . Break Effect increases the following by its percentage: Break DMG DoT/Delayed DMG dealt by inflicting Weakness Breaks Action delay caused by Quantum and Imaginary Weakness Breaks
