# Summon

**Type:** Combat

Summons are units that can be summoned by characters during battle. They are treated as individual units from their summoner, and will appear on the action order with their own fixed Speed stat. When they deal damage , they use the current stats of their characters. Generally, summons cannot be targeted by enemies or allies. Relics
